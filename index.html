Walk away, it locks.
Sit down, you're in.

iLand watches for you with the built-in camera. Leave and it locks; come back and it recognises you on the lock screen and signs you in.

  • Scan animation on the lock screen, right at the notch
  • Guided setup inside the island: the ring fills as you move your head
  • Calibrated matching across several frames, poor frames ignored
  • Runs fully on your Mac: a face fingerprint, never photos; password in your Keychain

Convenience, not security. A Mac camera can't see depth, so a good photo of you could fool it. Keep it off any Mac that holds anything you'd hate to lose.
